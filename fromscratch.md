From Scratch is where I try to make the world a little kinder, weirder, and smaller.

# [From Scratch Press](https://fromscratchpress.com/)

Through ridiculous essays, memoir, and fiction, I write about neurodivergence, meaningful work, and building a life that fits.

You can start with _[Why I left my 9-5 for good](https://fromscratchpress.com/why-i-left-my-9-5-for-good/)_, or check out my novella, _[Lake-Effect Coffee](https://fromscratchpress.com/books/)_, a workplace satire about friendship, burnout, and starting over with a coffee van.

# [From Scratch Code](https://fromscratchcode.com/)

I teach the craft of writing software from the ground up through mentorship and hands-on projects in Rust and Python. If you want to go deeper, I’d love to be your technical cheerleader.

I’m also building an open-source Python interpreter in Rust, known as [Memphis](https://fromscratchcode.com/memphis/), and sharing what I learn along the way.
