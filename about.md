Hi, I'm Tyler.

I'm a software engineer and technical mentor. At [From Scratch Code](https://fromscratchcode.com), I help quietly ambitious developers grow into strong engineers with more confidence and a calmer relationship to their craft.

My background spans software up-and-down the stack, including work at AMD and the MTA. That mix shapes how I think and teach: practical and grounded in how real systems behave.

Alongside mentoring, I build [Memphis](https://fromscratchcode.com/memphis/), an open-source Python interpreter written in Rust.

Before this chapter, I spent a decade working across public transportation and tech. To learn more, you can [visit the archive](/archive/).
