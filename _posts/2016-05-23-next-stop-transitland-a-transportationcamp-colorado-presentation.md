I attended the inaugural [TransportationCamp Colorado](http://transportationcamp.org/events/colorado-2016/) was last week! Sticking with the format of an "unconference," attendees were encouraged to propose their own sessions to present their work and/or lead discussions. I took them up on the format and presented the following slides on [Transitland](https://transit.land/) and how I used it to create my [New York City transit frequency visualization](/maps/new_york/). We had a really great interactive session with many good ideas exchanged!

Some stories and explanations were left off the slides. Feel free to hit me up for more explanation on anything!
